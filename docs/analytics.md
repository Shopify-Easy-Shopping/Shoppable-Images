# Analytics

The app records every hotspot a shopper opens, so you can see which images and which products are actually pulling attention.

**Analytics is included on every plan, including Free.** There is no limit on how many clicks are tracked or how far back the history goes.

## What Counts as a Click

A click is recorded when a shopper **opens a hotspot** — taps or clicks a pin and its product card appears.

Recorded with each click:

- which hotspot was opened
- your shop domain
- the timestamp

Nothing else. No customer identity, no IP address, no session or device information, no cookies. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy).

> **Note:** Clicks are only recorded for images that are actually live — published, and not locked by the Free plan.

## The Analytics Page

**Analytics** in the app navigation gives you a store-wide view.

### Overview tiles

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total clicks** | Every hotspot open across every image, all time |
| **Total images** | How many shoppable images you have, plus how many have received at least one click |
| **Total hotspots** | Every pin across every image |
| **Avg. clicks per image** | Total clicks divided by total images |

The *"N with at least 1 click"* figure under **Total images** is the one worth watching. An image with zero clicks is either not placed on a page shoppers reach, or has pins that are hard to notice.

### Images by performance

A table of every image ranked by click count, so your best and worst performers are immediately visible. Click through to any image for its own breakdown.

### Top hotspots

Your ten most-clicked individual hotspots across the whole store — the specific products shoppers keep tapping. Useful for deciding what to feature, restock, or push in a campaign.

## Per-Image Analytics

Open any image and choose its analytics view for a focused breakdown:

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total hotspot clicks** | All clicks on this image |
| **Active hotspots** | How many pins on this image have at least one click |
| **Hotspot breakdown** | A row per pin: product, label, click count, and when it was last clicked |

In the breakdown, a pin that has never been clicked shows **Never** in the *Last clicked* column. A cluster of *Never* rows usually means those pins are too small to notice, too close together on mobile, or sitting on a part of the image that gets cropped on narrow screens.

## What Analytics Does *Not* Track

Being clear about the limits so you can read the numbers correctly:

- **No impressions.** The app does not count how many times an image was *seen*, only how many times a pin was *opened*. You cannot calculate a click-through rate from these numbers alone.
- **No conversions or revenue.** The app does not know whether a click led to a purchase. To measure that, use Shopify's own analytics on the landing pages where your blocks appear.
- **No unique visitors.** Every open is counted. One shopper opening the same pin five times counts as five clicks.
- **No date filtering.** Totals are all-time. The per-hotspot *Last clicked* timestamp is the only time-based figure.

## Important Notes

> **Your own clicks count.** Previewing your storefront and tapping your own pins records real clicks. Test on a live page and your numbers will include your own testing. Clicks made inside the **Theme Editor** preview are not tracked.

> **Deleting removes history.** Deleting a hotspot deletes its click history. Deleting an image deletes the history for all of its hotspots. This cannot be undone — if you want a record, note the numbers down first.

> **Hiding does not remove history.** Turning **Published** off, or having an image locked by the Free plan, stops new clicks from being recorded but keeps everything already collected. Republish and the history is still there.

> **Editing a hotspot keeps its history.** Changing a hotspot's label or swapping its product keeps the same hotspot, so its click count carries over. If you want a clean count for a new product, delete the pin and add a new one.
