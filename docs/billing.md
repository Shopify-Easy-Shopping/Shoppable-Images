# Billing & Plans

The app has a permanently free plan and one paid plan, billed either monthly or annually. All billing is handled by Shopify and appears on your regular Shopify invoice.

## The Free Plan Limit — How It Works

This is the part worth understanding properly.

**The Free plan allows 3 *active* images.** You can create more than three, but only your **3 most recently created** images stay active. Everything older is marked **Locked**.

A locked image:

- **does not render on your storefront** — the block shows nothing
- **cannot be published** — the *Published* switch is disabled
- **keeps all its hotspots** — nothing is deleted
- **keeps its click history** — nothing is lost
- **is still fully editable** inside the app

The lock is applied automatically by the app. You cannot lock or unlock an image yourself — it is decided entirely by your plan and by which images are newest.

### What you will see

On the Free plan the Dashboard shows one of:

- **"Free plan: N of 3 images used"** — you still have room.
- **"Free plan limit reached (3 images)"** — you are at the cap.

Once you are at the cap, the **Create image** button is disabled, and trying to reach the create page sends you to the pricing page instead.

Locked images show a **Locked** badge on the images list, and their edit screen shows *"Only your 3 most recent images stay active on the Free plan."*

### Preview-only features

**Add to cart** and **Masonry layout** can be switched on in the Theme Editor on the Free plan, and you will see them working **in the Theme Editor preview**. They do not take effect for real shoppers on your live store until you upgrade. See [Theme Blocks](theme-blocks#plan-gated-settings).

## Upgrading

1. Go to **Pricing** in the app navigation.
2. Choose **Ultimate Monthly** or **Ultimate Annual** and click **Upgrade**.
3. Shopify shows its own approval screen with the charge details.
4. Click **Approve**.

You are returned to the pricing page and the new plan is active immediately.

**What happens the moment you upgrade:**

- Every locked image is **unlocked** at once, with all of its hotspots intact.
- The image limit is removed — create as many as you want.
- **Add to cart** and **Masonry** become active on your live storefront, using whatever block settings you already saved. No theme changes are needed.

> **Note:** Unlocking restores images to their previous **Published** state. An image you had deliberately hidden before it was locked stays hidden — turn it back on from its edit screen.

## Switching Between Monthly and Annual

You can switch in either direction at any time.

1. Go to **Pricing**.
2. On the plan you want, click **Switch to this plan**.
3. Approve the new charge in Shopify.

Shopify handles the changeover and prorates the existing subscription according to its standard billing rules. There is no interruption to your features — you stay on Ultimate throughout.

## Downgrading to Free

1. Go to **Pricing**.
2. Click **Downgrade to Free**.
3. Confirm the prompt: *"Downgrade to the Free plan? You will lose access to unlimited images."*

Your subscription is cancelled and your shop returns to the Free plan.

**What happens when you downgrade:**

- Your **3 most recently created** images stay active.
- **Every other image is locked** — hidden from your storefront, but not deleted. All hotspots and all click history are kept.
- **Add to cart** and **Masonry** stop working on your live store. They stay switched on in your block settings and keep working in the Theme Editor preview.
- Analytics continues to work in full.

> **Nothing is ever deleted by a downgrade.** Locking is fully reversible: upgrade again and every locked image comes straight back, exactly as it was, with its hotspots and history. You never have to rebuild anything.

> **Which three stay active is decided by creation date**, not by which you consider most important. If you plan to run on Free long-term, that is worth keeping in mind — the three you keep are simply the three you made most recently.

## Billing FAQ

**Can I switch plans at any time?**
Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or move between monthly and annual whenever you like, from the Pricing page.

**What happens to my images if I downgrade?**
Nothing is deleted. Your 3 most recent images stay active; the rest are locked and stop showing on your storefront until you upgrade again.

**Will I get my images back if I upgrade again?**
Yes — immediately, and complete with their hotspots and click history.

**Is there a trial?**
The Free plan is permanent and unlimited in time. It is not a trial that expires; the only limit is 3 active images.

**How am I billed?**
Through Shopify. Charges appear on your normal Shopify invoice — the app never handles your payment details.

**Do hotspots count towards any limit?**
No. Hotspots are unlimited on every plan, including Free. Only the number of *images* is limited.

**Is analytics limited on the Free plan?**
No. Click tracking, the Analytics page, per-image breakdowns, and full history are identical on both plans.

**Do I need to change my theme after upgrading?**
No. Unlocked images and the plan-gated block settings take effect automatically.
